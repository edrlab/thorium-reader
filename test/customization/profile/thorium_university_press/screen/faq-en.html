Frequently asked questions

Answers to common questions about the library and its services.

LibraryQuestions and answers

01What are the library's opening hours?›

The library is open Monday to Friday, 9 a.m. to 6 p.m. Hours may vary during university holidays.

02How can I borrow a book?›

Present your library card at the circulation desk or use a self-service kiosk. Your account must be in good standing.

03What is the loan duration?›

The standard loan period is four weeks. Some reference materials are available for in-library use only.

04How can I renew a loan?›

Renew items through your online account or ask a staff member, provided no one else has placed a hold.

05Are there late fees for overdue books?›

Overdue items may temporarily suspend borrowing privileges. Contact the circulation desk if you need assistance.

06How can I access online resources?›

Sign in with your university credentials through the library portal to access databases and journals.

07Can I reserve a study room?›

Yes. Study rooms can be reserved through the library portal, subject to availability.

08What are the rules for using computer stations?›

Computer stations are for academic work. Please follow the usage guidelines and sign out when you finish.

09How can I contact the library staff?›

Visit the information desk or use the contact form on the university website.

10Where can I find research documents and theses?›

Search the online catalog or ask staff for help with the research collections.
